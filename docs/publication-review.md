## Authorization and Scope

The owner requested a public pedagogical research archive and Zenodo record,
approved MIT original code and CC BY 4.0 new notes, and instructed us to proceed
after reviewing the local candidate. Keep the existing repository name.
After public release verification, mark GitHub read-only. The local checkout
remains in place for the owner's later move.

The release preserves three bounded comparisons, eight reconstructed tables,
and one local teaching example. Counts are reconstructed from saved individual
outcomes. They are not fresh inference or independent symbolic rescoring.
The proposed full off-policy RL/search efficiency study was not completed.
The existing broader test limitations are carried forward unchanged.
