\caption{An episode over the worked forest of \cref{sec:method-state}: solve
$|x{-}3| + |x{+}2| = 7$ by exhaustive cases, asked for the largest solution.
Circles are forest nodes: $G$ goals, $F$ facts, $H$ hypotheses; green with a
check mark means verifier-vouched (a \textsc{VerifierCall} succeeded), thick
green means committed, gray means abandoned (a forward prune that retains
history), and white means open. Each panel is one immutable state; the
arrows between panels name the $(\textit{verb}, \textit{target})$ actions
applied. Structure never gates: $G_2$ contributes nothing and is abandoned,
yet the root commits on the aggregate of its two verified branches, and only
that root \textsc{Commit} is terminal and verifier-gated, yielding the
episode's sole task reward $r_T = +1$.}
\label{fig:episode-timeline}
