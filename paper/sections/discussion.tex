\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What is and is not claimed.} Every element of this system has a
precedent somewhere: small external policies over frozen LMs
\citep{rlprompt, tempera}, external value-based selection over a frozen
LLM's proposals \citep{dialogxpert}, operation-level MDPs \citep{gsos},
off-policy value-based RL for LLM agents \citep{archer, oreo}, verifiable
reward \citep{deepseekr1, tulu3}, pointer selection over dynamic sets
\citep{pointernets}, GNN encodings of proof-like states
\citep{paliwal2020graph, graph2tac}, and teacher bootstrapping
\citep{dqfd, alphago}. The claim is the conjunction, an external
under-5M-parameter controller with a factored verb-plus-pointer action
space over a growing reasoning forest, trained by off-policy Q-learning
against a symbolic-verifier-only reward, with lens-scoped generation and
MCTS strictly additive, and the compute-frontier consequence of that
conjunction. A reviewer who asks ``which single mechanism is new?'' will
find neighbors for each; we believe the honest form of the contribution is
architectural, and \cref{sec:related} names the nearest neighbor on every
axis, including concurrent work on learned context curation
\citep{contextcuration}.

\paragraph{Is the combination just engineering?} The strongest version of
the objection is that each ingredient is published, so assembling them is
integration work rather than research. Two answers, one conceptual and
one empirical. Conceptually, the elements are not independent modules:
the pointer head presupposes a state whose nodes are stable and typed,
the lens presupposes that acting and scoping are the same decision, and
the verifier-only reward is only trainable at all because the typed
action space gives credit assignment something to bind to; remove any one
element and the others lose their function, which is the signature of an
architecture rather than a pipeline. Empirically, the formative campaign
(\cref{sec:preliminary}) shows the elements interact non-additively:
raising controller capability roughly $5\times$ moved nothing while the
observation carried the structure poorly (1/20 in both cells of
\cref{tab:2x2}), and restoring the structural rendering at fixed
capability moved outcomes four- to five-fold. A combination whose value
were merely additive engineering would not produce that interaction
pattern; the structured state, the typed actions, and the observation
design succeed or fail jointly, which is precisely the claim.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
The verifier boundary is the trust boundary: the system is only as sound as
the symbolic checker and the discipline that the check's expected value is
never supplied by the policy. The Markov bet is explicit: the forest is a
lossy, parser-mediated compression of the reasoning history. The domain is
verifiable math by construction; extending the reward invariant to domains
without symbolic checkers is future work, not a small step. Small frozen
generators bound attainable content quality at any controller strength.
\stub{empirical limitations, once results exist}

\paragraph{Outlook.} The design admits three extensions recorded but not
claimed: a graph-neural encoder as the principled structural embedding, a
retrieval verb whose content comes from a corpus rather than the LLM, and
proof-level verification in which connective aggregation and exhaustiveness
obligations become checked rather than advisory.
