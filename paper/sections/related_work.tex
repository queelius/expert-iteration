\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

We organize prior work by the axis on which it is closest to this system.
Across the surveyed literature, essentially every strong reasoning-RL result
applies reinforcement learning to the language model's own weights at token
granularity over a flat trace; the systems below are the nearest neighbors
on each individual element of our design, and none combines them.

\paragraph{Search-guided reasoning with learned values.}
TS-LLM \citep{tsllm} is the closest structural analogue: an
AlphaZero-style \citep{alphazero} generalized-policy-iteration loop for LLM
reasoning, with PUCT MCTS guided by a learned value function and policy
distillation from search. It differs from this work on the two axes we take
to be load-bearing: its reward is a \emph{trained} outcome reward model, and
the object being improved is the LLM itself, fine-tuned; our reward is a
symbolic verifier with no learned model anywhere in the reward path, our
controller is an external network of a few million parameters, and MCTS is
optional rather than the spine. RAP \citep{rap} and LATS \citep{lats} run
MCTS over LLM reasoning with the LLM itself serving as world model and value
estimator through prompting, with no trained component. rStar-Math
\citep{rstarmath} and ReST-MCTS* \citep{restmcts} anchor search on learned
process preference or process reward models and self-train the generator;
we take the harder line that only a symbolic verifier produces reward.
MCTSr \citep{mctsr} and Agent Q \citep{agentq} use LLM self-evaluation or
self-critique as intermediate reward, which is exactly the LLM-judge signal
we exclude (\cref{sec:method-reward}).

\paragraph{Operation-level action spaces.}
Guided Stream of Search \citep{gsos} formulates reasoning as an
operation-level MDP in which each discrete action is a search operation,
and validates operation-level over token-level credit assignment; it
distills guidance into the generating model by supervised fine-tuning.
Searchformer \citep{searchformer} internalizes A*-style search traces into
a transformer by imitation and expert iteration, the precedent for a model
that runs no explicit search at deployment (our $B{=}0$ mode). Tree of
Thoughts \citep{tot} and Graph of Thoughts \citep{got} structure
intermediate reasoning as trees and graphs with propose/evaluate/expand
operations, but select operations by prompted heuristics rather than a
trained controller. Our $(\textit{verb}, \textit{target})$ action space is
an operation-level MDP in the GSoS sense, with the addition of an explicit
pointer over the live nodes of a growing forest, a factorization with, to
our knowledge, no precedent in LLM-reasoning RL; pointer-style selection
over dynamic sets originates outside this setting \citep{pointernets}, as
does the typed-temporally-extended-action view of the options framework
\citep{options}.

\paragraph{Value-based RL for LLM systems.}
Nearly all reasoning-RL training is policy-gradient (PPO/GRPO
\citep{deepseekr1, tulu3}) or preference-based (DPO variants), consistent
with the surveys of the current recipe landscape. The value-based exceptions
train the LLM itself: ArCHer \citep{archer} runs hierarchical off-policy
value-based RL for multi-turn LLM agents over the model's token policy;
OREO \citep{oreo} trains the LLM's policy jointly with a value network under
a soft Bellman objective, offline, with terminal-only rule-based reward, and
uses the value to guide step-level beam search at inference, empirical
support for both of our core bets (value-based training under sparse
terminal reward, and value-guided test-time search), made while optimizing
the generator's own weights; ROVER \citep{rover} derives an RLVR method from
Q-values of a fixed random policy, again training the LLM. SWEET-RL
\citep{sweetrl} learns a step-level critic with privileged information for
multi-turn agents, optimized with DPO. We keep the generator frozen and
place the entire learned capacity in an external controller small enough
that its per-decision cost is negligible next to one LLM forward pass.

\paragraph{External modules over frozen LLMs.}
RLPrompt \citep{rlprompt} and TEMPERA \citep{tempera} are the direct
ancestors of our architecture on one axis: a small external policy trained
with RL over a \emph{frozen} language model. Their action spaces are
single-turn prompt tokens or discrete prompt edits for classification-style
tasks; ours is a multi-step MDP of typed reasoning operations over an
evolving structured state. PNLC \citep{pnlc} learns an offline
goal-conditioned value critic that guides a frozen LLM's action refinement
without any external action policy; we learn the action policy itself, and
anchor its value on a symbolic verifier rather than a learned critic. UniR
\citep{unir} attaches an external reasoning module to a frozen LLM at the
logit level with supervised verifiable-reward guidance; our module acts at
the operation level via off-policy Q-learning. DSPy \citep{dspy} optimizes
LM-program orchestration without RL or a learned controller network. In
concurrent work, Active Context Curation \citep{contextcuration} trains a
lightweight RL policy (a 7B model) to prune the working memory of a frozen
executor; this is the closest existing work on our lens axis, and differs in
that our context scoping is not a dedicated pruning policy but a byproduct
of typed reasoning operations chosen by an under-5M-parameter controller,
and in the verifier-anchored reward.

\paragraph{Verifiable rewards and verification-in-the-loop.}
DeepSeek-R1 \citep{deepseekr1} and T\"ulu 3 \citep{tulu3} established
RLVR at scale, and R1's negative finding on learned process-reward models
(reward hacking, ill-defined step boundaries) is one of the two pillars of
our reward design; the other is CRITIC \citep{critic}, which shows LLM
self-critique is unreliable without external grounding. V-STaR
\citep{vstar} trains a verifier and STaR \citep{star} bootstraps from
self-generated rationales, both placing learned components where we require
a symbolic check. AlphaGeometry \citep{alphageometry} and AlphaProof
demonstrate that hard-verifier-anchored systems with tiny operation
vocabularies reach the strongest reliability, which motivated our
aggressive verb-set minimization. Self-Backtracking \citep{selfbacktrack}
internalizes pruning as a learned, emittable action, the precedent for our
\textsc{Abandon} verb surviving at $B{=}0$ where no search controller
exists. GSM8K \citep{gsm8k} and MATH \citep{math} supply the verifiable
benchmarks of our evaluation ladder.

\paragraph{Positioning.} The precise conjunction this paper claims, a tiny
external controller (neither the LLM nor an LLM-sized critic), a factored
verb-plus-pointer action space over a growing reasoning forest, off-policy
value-based Q-learning as the training spine with MCTS strictly additive, a
symbolic verifier as the sole reward, and lens-scoped generation, appears in
no prior work we are aware of, individually well-precedented as each element
is. \Cref{sec:discussion} returns to this honestly.
